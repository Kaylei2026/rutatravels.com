# Ruta Travels

> Ruta Travels is a privacy-first travel product launching later this year. We publish hand-curated city travel guides and editorial travel writing. Our editorial standard is that every recommendation is written by a person who has been to the place, not scraped or generated.

## About

Ruta is being built around 24 hand-curated city guides, each written and edited by people with first-hand knowledge of the city. Our positioning is the Fodor's of digital guides: editorial authority, curated content, privacy as differentiator. We do not track readers, log location, or build behavioral profiles.

## Journal

The Ruta journal covers the full trip lifecycle: dreaming and discovery, planning and booking, packing and preparation, transit and layovers, on-the-ground exploration, and reflection after the trip.

- [Why we hand-curate 24 city travel guides](https://rutatravels.com/blog/why-we-hand-curate-travel-guides/): Our editorial philosophy and why scraped content fails travelers.
- [How to plan a three-stop European trip without losing your mind](https://rutatravels.com/blog/three-stop-european-trip-planning/): A six-step framework for multi-city Europe itineraries.
- [Off-season Lisbon: what locals actually do in November](https://rutatravels.com/blog/off-season-lisbon-november/): The Lisbon locals love, hidden by summer crowds.
- [A perfect day in Mexico City's Roma Norte](https://rutatravels.com/blog/perfect-day-roma-norte-mexico-city/): An hour-by-hour walking guide to one of Mexico City's best neighborhoods.
