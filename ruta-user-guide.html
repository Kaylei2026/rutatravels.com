Getting started

Welcome to Ruta

Ruta keeps your whole trip in one place and stays with you from planning through landing, tracking your flights, predicting disruptions before they hit, and turning into a local guide when you arrive.

The basics

  • Your trip moves through phases: Plan, Upcoming, Today, En Route, and Post-trip. Ruta shows what matters for the phase you're in.
  • Add a flight to get started, everything else builds around it.
  • Tap the ? on any screen for help with that exact feature.

Add a flight

Get your flight into Ruta in whatever way is easiest.

Ways to add

  • Search by flight number or route.
  • Scan a boarding pass or a screenshot/PDF of your itinerary.
  • Paste the text of a confirmation email.
  • Share to Ruta directly from your airline app, Mail, or Photos.
  • Enter by hand, type your flight legs.
Sharing the confirmation text or a clear screenshot works best. A plain link usually can't be read because it sits behind a login.
Plan

Your trip timeline

Flights, hotels, rental cars, trains, and activities live together on one journey timeline, in order.

How to use

  • Add hotels, cars, trains, and activities from your trip view; each shows up at the right point in the journey.
  • Saved details (confirmation numbers, times, addresses) surface when they're relevant, including on arrival day.

Multi-leg trips & connections

Ruta understands connections and watches the tight ones.

How to use

  • Add each leg, Ruta tracks them as one journey and flags connection risk if an inbound delay threatens your next flight.
  • When you land, the next leg activates automatically.

Packing checklist

A customizable checklist, organized by category, so nothing gets left behind.

How to use

  • Find it in Journey under Prepare. Tap items to check them off.
  • Add your own items with the Add button; they stay with this trip.
  • Remove a custom item with the X next to it.

Pre-trip checklist & reminders

Critical to-dos (passport, visa, medications, and more) with an optional night-before nudge.

How to use

  • Check off critical items as you handle them.
  • Turn on Pre-trip Reminders in Notifications to get a single night-before reminder for anything still unchecked.

Travel documents

Store your passport, visa, and trusted-traveler details, and get reminded before anything expires.

How to use

  • Add document numbers and pick expiry dates with the date picker.
  • Turn on expiry reminders to be nudged a year, six months, and a month ahead.
Document numbers are stored encrypted on your device (in the iOS Keychain) and are never sent to our servers. Keep a photo of your passport saved separately, too.

Destination briefing

An AI-generated rundown for where you're headed: what to pack, customs limits, health notes, culture, and know-before-you-go basics like plugs, water, and tipping.

AI-generated, so double-check anything critical against an official source.

Visa & entry requirements

A starting point on what you may need to enter your destination, with embassy contacts by citizenship.

Travel requirements change. Always confirm with the destination's embassy or consulate before booking, this is a starting point, not authoritative legal guidance.

City guides

Curated things to do and see, organized so you can plan a day without the overwhelm.

How to use

  • Browse by city; tap a place for details and tap Navigate to open it in Maps.
Travel day & airport

Live flight tracking

Real-time status, gate, terminal, and progress for your flight.

How to use

  • Open your flight to see live status; Ruta refreshes more often as departure nears.
Gate and time are confirmed by your airline at the gate, always glance at the airport boards too.

Gate, delay & departure alerts

Push notifications when your gate changes, your flight is delayed, or it's leaving earlier than planned.

How to use

  • Turn the alerts you want on in Notifications. They fire automatically while a trip is active.

Trip risk check

A one-tap scan across your flights for delays and connection risk, with a clear all-clear when you're good.

How to use

  • Tap the risk card on Today to run the scan; tap a flagged item to see what to do.

When to leave

A suggested departure time for the airport based on live traffic.

Live traffic can shift, double-check before you head out.

Airport: maps, TSA, lounges

Terminal maps and gate navigation, TSA wait times, amenities, and lounge access detection.

How to use

  • Open the Airport view for your terminal's map, security wait estimates, food and shops, and lounges you can access.
Wait times are estimates based on typical patterns; check the airport screens on arrival.
In-flight & arrival

In-flight & connections

Connection awareness while you're in the air, with alerts if a tight connection is at risk, and your next leg ready the moment you land.

Arrival hub

When you land, Ruta surfaces what you need on the ground: your saved hotel, rental car, or train for the day, baggage claim, and a one-tap ride.

How to use

  • On Today after landing, tap your saved car/train/hotel to see details, or tap Uber/Lyft to get a ride toward your destination.
  • Tap Baggage claim for where to find your bags.

Weather, currency & translation

Local weather, a currency converter set to your home currency, key phrases with pronunciation, and free-form translation, plus cultural tips and emergency numbers.

How to use

  • Open the Destination tab once you've arrived; tools adapt to where you are.
  • Use Show to Locals to display a translated phrase to someone.
After the trip

Expenses

Log trip spending as you go and review it later.

How to use

  • Add an expense with amount and category; review the totals in your recap.

Trip recap & archive

When your trip wraps, Ruta shifts to a recap and tucks the trip into your archive so you can start the next one fresh.

Setup & account

Voice assistant

Ask about your trip, gate, status, or weather, hands-free.

You're talking to Ruta's AI assistant, not a person. Double-check anything critical.

Languages

Ruta is available in nine languages. Set yours in Settings, and the app, including voice, follows.

Notifications

Choose exactly which alerts you get: gate changes, delays, earlier departures, connection risk, boarding, when-to-leave, document expiry, and the night-before pre-trip reminder.

How to use

  • Toggle each notification type in Settings under Notifications.

Offline mode

Your saved trip works without a signal. Ruta shows your trip and last-synced info, and updates the live pieces when you reconnect.

Download offline maps for your destination in Apple or Google Maps so navigation works on the ground too.

Privacy & your data

Your trip and sensitive documents stay on your device. Document numbers are encrypted in the device Keychain and are never sent to our servers.

Good to know

  • Some features use AI and live data; those are labeled, and we point you to official sources for anything critical.
  • Read the full Privacy Policy.
