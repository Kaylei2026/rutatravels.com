Ruta Travels press kit

Your whole trip in one calm view, with your back when the day doesn't go to plan. Everything a writer needs to cover Ruta is on this page.

Boilerplate

Ruta Travels is a calm travel companion for iPhone. It keeps a traveler's whole trip in one view, flights, hotels, cars, trains, and a real guide to each city, and it watches every flight so a delay or a gate change reaches you early, while there is still time to act. Ruta is built to have your back when a trip does not go to plan, and to do it without turning your journey into a data trail: your itinerary and travel documents stay on your device. It is a companion for the trip you already booked, wherever you booked it, rather than another place to book.

Ruta is free to start, with a premium subscription that unlocks curated day-by-day city guides, AI destination briefings, and full disruption guidance. It is available now on the App Store.

What Ruta does

  • Keeps your whole trip in one timeline: flights, hotels, cars, trains, and connections.
  • Watches every flight and tells you early when something changes, with what it means and your next move.
  • Flags tight connections and disruption risk across the whole journey, not just one leg.
  • Tells you when to leave for the airport, with traffic and security worked in.
  • Includes a real, hand-written guide to each destination, plus a day-by-day schedule.
  • Turns a finished trip into a shareable Trip Recap, a map and stats built from what you already entered.
  • Keeps your passport, visa, and traveler numbers encrypted on the device, behind Face ID.
