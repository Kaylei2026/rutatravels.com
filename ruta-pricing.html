Things worth knowing

What is the founders rate and how do I qualify?
The founders rate is the best price Ruta will ever be. Start your free trial during the launch window and you qualify, whether you convert from trial to monthly or annual. Your rate stays locked for as long as you remain subscribed. If you cancel and resubscribe after the window closes, standard pricing applies.
What is the 7-day free trial?
New subscribers get 7 days of full access before their first charge. Start it while you are planning your trip rather than mid-journey, so you get a real feel for Ruta before the day that matters. You can cancel any time during the trial and you will not be charged.
Can I switch from monthly to annual?
Yes. If you start on Founders Monthly and switch to Founders Annual while the founders window is still open, your founders rate carries over to the annual plan.
How do I cancel?
Through your iPhone Settings under Subscriptions. Find Ruta Travels, tap Cancel Subscription. Access continues until the end of your current billing period and you will not be charged again.
What happens when the founders window closes?
Standard pricing replaces the founders rates for new subscribers. Standard monthly is $11.99 and standard annual is around $79.99. If you are already subscribed, your founders rate does not change. The window exists because we want our earliest users to have something real for believing in Ruta early.
